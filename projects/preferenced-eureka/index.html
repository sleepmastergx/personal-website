Preference-based reinforcement learning with language and visual feedback

An agentic RL pipeline connecting language task specifications to reward models and PyTorch training, with automated preferences extracted from rollout videos for learning in low-feedback settings.

Researcher · Learning, Embodied Autonomy, and Forecasting (LEAF) Lab
November 2024–July 2026 · University of Toronto

Approach

The pipeline brings together language task specifications, rollout data, preference labels, reward models, and policy training. LLMs and vision-language models extract preferences from rollout videos to support preference-based reinforcement learning when feedback is limited.

My contributions

  • Built the agentic RL pipeline connecting language task specifications to reward models and PyTorch training.
  • Standardized data schemas for instructions, rollouts, and preference labels.
  • Automated LLM/VLM preference extraction over rollout videos for preference-based RL in low-feedback settings.
  • Implemented exploration baselines in Isaac Sim and added logging to training and data-extraction pipelines.
  • Worked with Prof. Nicholas Rhinehart on prototype integration and evaluation.

Tools & setting

PyTorch for training, Isaac Sim for exploration baselines, and language and vision-language models for preference extraction.
